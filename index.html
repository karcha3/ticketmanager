Built for keeping tickets organized

Every detail in a row

Name, venue type, ticket type, venue or event, location, date, time, seat or section, price, status and notes.

Instant search

Filters every field as you type, within the tab you’re viewing.

A tab for every venue

Tabs are created automatically from the venues you enter, and update as you add or edit tickets.

Calendar and clock pickers

Choose dates from a calendar popup and times from a clock dial or 30-minute slots.

Duplicate for group buys

Buying several seats? Duplicate a ticket, change the name and seat, and save.

Export what you see

Export CSV respects your search and the active venue tab, so you can export everything or just one event.

Your data stays on your PC

Tickets are stored in a local SQLite database in your user data folder. Nothing is uploaded.

Light and dark mode

Switch themes with one click. The app remembers your choice.
