Built for keeping tickets organized

Every detail in a row

Name, venue type, ticket type, venue or event, location, date, time, seat or section, price, status (Active, Used, Cancelled or Refunded) and notes.

Instant search

Filters every field as you type, within the tab you’re viewing.

A tab for every venue

Tabs are created automatically from the venues you enter, and update as you add or edit tickets.

Calendar and clock pickers

Choose dates from a calendar popup and times from a clock dial or in 30-minute steps.

Duplicate for group buys

Buying several seats? Duplicate a ticket, change the name and seat, and save.

Total value at a glance

The status bar shows your ticket count and the total price of your tickets, not counting cancelled or refunded ones.

Export what you see

Export CSV follows your search and the active venue tab, so you can export everything or just one venue.

Your data stays on your PC

Tickets are stored in a local database on your computer and are not uploaded anywhere.

Light and dark mode

Switch themes with one click. The app remembers your choice.
