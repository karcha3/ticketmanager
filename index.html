All your event tickets in one list.

Ticket Manager is a Windows app for tracking the tickets you hold: who they’re for, which event, where you’re sitting, what they cost and their status.

A simple table for every ticket you own

Everything about a ticket, in one row

All the details

Name, venue type, ticket type, event, location, date, time, seat or section, price and status.

Search everything

Find a ticket by name, venue, location or status as you type.

Tabs by venue

Switch between all venues or view just one kind of event.

Add, edit, duplicate

Duplicate a ticket when you buy several for the same show, then change what differs.

Know what it’s worth

The status bar shows your ticket count and the total value of active tickets.

Export to CSV

Send your list to Excel or Google Sheets in one click.

Up and running in three steps

  1. Add a ticket

    Enter the event, seat, date and price.

  2. Organize

    Duplicate, edit or search to keep the list accurate.

  3. Export

    Save your list as a CSV whenever you need a copy.
