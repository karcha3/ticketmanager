Privacy & cookies

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This page explains what happens to information when you visit this website or use the Ticket Manager app, both provided by Karcha Studios.

This website

The website has no accounts, no forms, no comments and no advertising. It does not ask you for any personal information, and we do not collect any ourselves. It does not load fonts, scripts, videos or other content from outside services. The only outside requests are the links you choose to click.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics or tracking tools, and does not store anything in your browser. Because of that, there is no cookie banner to accept or decline. If we ever add anything that needs your consent, we will ask first and update this page.

Hosting and downloads

The website and the app downloads are hosted by GitHub. When you open this site or download a file, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. We do not receive that information from the website itself.

The Ticket Manager app

The tickets you enter (for example names, events, seats and prices) are saved only on your own computer, in a database in the folder %APPDATA%\TicketManager. Settings such as your light or dark theme are also saved on your computer. The app does not send your tickets or settings to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them.

You are responsible for your own backups (the Export CSV button helps). If you enter other people’s details, such as the name on a ticket, please handle them as the law in your country requires. Uninstalling the app does not delete your ticket data. You can delete that folder yourself at any time.
